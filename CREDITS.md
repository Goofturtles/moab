# Credits

## People photos

The seven people in this demo are made up, and so are their companies (Northwind Freight, Quillstack, Rookery Retail, Helix Health, Lumen Air). The faces are stock photos of real models used only to make the demo look like a real tool. They are not those people, and the photos say nothing about the people shown.

The photos are hotlinked from the Pexels CDN (`images.pexels.com`, allowed in `img-src` in `_headers`), not stored here. If one fails to load, the avatar falls back to initials. Chris is a real person, so he never gets a stock face: his avatar is a "C" monogram.
